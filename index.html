Welcome to LifeLink

We started this project to make it easier for willing donors to connect with hospitals and learn how to take their next step. Every donor registration helps our team understand who may be available when a local need arises.

Registering does not guarantee eligibility or an appointment. A qualified donation centre will confirm the next steps with you.
